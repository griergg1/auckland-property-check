# morebeanz — product marketing context

Single source of truth for any copywriting or positioning work on morebeanz.
Distilled from `docs/BRIEF.md`, `docs/MARKET_RESEARCH.md`, `docs/COMPETITOR_Duly.md`,
and `progress.md`. Keep this short; update it when the product or positioning

## What morebeanz is (one line)

A free Auckland property check you run on a section before you pay for the
LIM, the builder, or the lawyer.

## Who it's for

Auckland buyers shortlisting a standalone house or a vacant section, who plan
to **live in it, renovate, extend, add a dwelling, or build on vacant land**.

Not for (this version): apartments, cross-leases, and properties with
ambiguous boundaries.

## The one transformation

From "I'm about to offer and I don't know what's on or under this section" →
"Here's what the council maps say about this section, in plain English, with
the source link next to every line."

## What we sell (the offer)

- **Free now:** type an address, confirm the right section, read the findings
  on screen, with sources.
- **Planned (not built):** a printable PDF report at NZ$25–30, positioned as a
  pre-LIM screen. The Auckland Council LIM is $375 / 10 working days. The
  paywall is not built; nothing is charged today.

## The five differentiators (say these out loud, in customer words)

1. **We check the section, not the street.** Every flood and planning line
   is matched to the actual land parcel, not a 500 m circle around the
   address. The finding is about your section, not your neighbourhood.
2. **Ordered for what you're actually doing.** Renovating, extending, adding
   a dwelling, or building on vacant land — the check that matters most to
   *that* decision comes first.
3. **We don't say "all clear" when we don't know.** Every finding is one of
   four things: yes, no, we couldn't tell, or it needs a human to look. If
   the data's missing, we say so — we never call a section safe just because
   the map was blank.
4. **No AI wrote this.** Findings come from deterministic rules against the
   council's own maps, with the source link and the date we pulled it. The
   report can't invent a flood that isn't there, or miss one that is.
5. **Council rules, with the rule book linked.** The zone check names the
   zone and links to the exact chapter of the Unitary Plan that sets what
   you can do on it.

(Plus two operational ones: per-finding "what this map does NOT tell you"
lines, and we pull the council layers straight from the council's own data
hub under a free-to-reuse licence.)

## What morebeanz is NOT (never claim these)

- Not a LIM. Order one from Auckland Council before you commit.
- Not ownership / title confirmation. The parcel match is geometry, not a
  title.
- Not a building inspection, a weathertightness or meth test, or a
  registered valuation.
- Not a guarantee you'll get consent. The zone check names the base zone;
  overlays, precincts, designations, and a full application decide that.
- Not a flood / insurance / floor-level answer.

## Voice and tone

- **Plain English, no jargon.** "The section" not "the parcel polygon."
  "Council flood maps" not "Auckland Council overland flow paths." "What
  you can build" not "Unitary Plan base zone."
- **Talk to the buyer, not to ourselves.** "You" not "the user." Short
  sentences. One idea per line.
- **Honest over hype.** No exclamation marks, no "amazing," no invented
  numbers. We say what we don't know out loud.
- **Warm, not cold.** This is the biggest purchase of someone's life. The
  tone should feel like a careful friend who knows the maps, not a spec
  sheet.
- **Specific over vague.** "$375 / 10 working days" not "expensive and
  slow." "1,163 m²" not "a decent-sized section."

## Positioning vs the market

- **vs Auckland Council GeoMaps:** same data, but we read it for you and
  tell you what it means for your decision, in plain English.
- **vs $49 hazard PDFs (Know Your Risk, SiteLenz):** we're parcel-pinned
  and intention-aware, not a one-size hazard score.
- **vs Duly (AI reports):** our findings are deterministic and source-cited,
  not AI-generated. We can't hallucinate; they explicitly hedge that they
  might.
- **vs the LIM:** we're the screen *before* the LIM, not a replacement.
